Identification invariant — what makes counterfactual deployment honest
Training uses the free-flow tij(m) throughout the inverse loop, so βt,m is identified as the sensitivity of utility to free-flow time — no flow ↔ time simultaneity in MLE. Deployment substitutes BPR-equilibrium tij,t(car), eq into the same trained βt,m. Substitution is consistent under single-component invariance (Schölkopf et al., 2021): the choice mechanism (β, γ, δ, κ) is structural and stable; only its inputs change at deployment.

Why 33,026 ML weights vs 77 behavioural primitives? The encoder Θ(1,2,3) learns a flexible non-parametric Vj,t from data, soaking up everything the simple gravity model gets wrong about spatial attractiveness. The 77 RUM coefficients in ④ are then identified conditional on V, free to carry behavioural meaning instead of fitting V's noise. The paper's contribution is recovering those 77 — the 33k are the supporting representation, not the contribution.
